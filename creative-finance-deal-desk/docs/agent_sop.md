# Agent SOP

## Daily flow
1. Review hot leads first
2. Work Day-1 follow-ups (leads from yesterday)
3. Work Day-3 follow-ups
4. Work Day-7 follow-ups
5. Score and enrich any partially qualified leads
6. Flag any compliance concerns to the closer

## When a lead arrives
1. Confirm identity and channel preference
2. Ask the next best question (from the scored lead output)
3. Log facts into the CRM
4. Re-run the scoring workflow
5. Set the next follow-up touch

## When a lead goes hot
1. Attempt live contact within 60 minutes
2. Book a time with a closer
3. Do not discuss contract structure yourself
4. Hand off with the generated closer packet

## When a lead goes cold
1. Send a low-pressure re-engagement message
2. Park in long-cycle follow-up (Day 30 touch)
3. Note the reason in the lead notes
