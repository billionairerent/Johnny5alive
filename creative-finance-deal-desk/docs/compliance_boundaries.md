# Compliance Boundaries

The system must operate within these boundaries at all times.

## Hard rules
1. Do not promise financing.
2. Do not provide legal advice.
3. Do not draft contracts.
4. Do not advise users to hide or misrepresent information.
5. Do not describe the system as a lender, broker, or law firm.

## Escalation triggers
Mark a lead with `ESCALATE TO PROFESSIONAL` when any of the following appear:
- foreclosure, pre-foreclosure, bankruptcy
- due-on-sale clause handling
- title disputes or lien concerns
- unclear ownership
- note creation / servicing / Dodd-Frank questions
- licensing or lending-rule questions
- minors, incapacity, guardianship
- explicit requests for legal or lending advice

## Language rules
- Use plain language in lead-facing scripts.
- Avoid "guaranteed", "approved", "pre-approved", "we will finance", "lender".
- Prefer "talk with an owner directly", "explore flexible terms", "discuss options".
