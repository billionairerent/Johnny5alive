# Skill: Deal Matching

## Purpose
Compare a seller lead and a buyer lead to assess fit.

## Match Criteria
- geography: buyer target area overlaps seller property area
- budget vs. asking price (within reasonable range)
- down payment vs. likely terms
- timeline alignment
- buyer flexibility matches seller flexibility

## Fit Score (0-10)
- 9-10: strong fit, ready to package
- 6-8: workable fit with noted gaps
- 3-5: weak fit, likely needs another pairing
- 0-2: no fit

## Output
- fitScore
- reasonsForFit
- missingInformation
- riskFlags
- recommendedNextStep
