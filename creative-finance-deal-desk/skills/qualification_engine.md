# Skill: Qualification Engine

## Purpose
Convert raw lead data into a qualified status by confirming enough data points exist to advance the lead.

## Required Seller Data Points
- asking price
- mortgage status (if any)
- timeline
- openness to flexible terms
- occupancy

## Required Buyer Data Points
- target area
- budget range
- down payment available
- timeline
- monthly income (approximate)

## Qualification Tiers
- Qualified: all required data points present and coherent
- Partially Qualified: 3-4 of 5 present
- Unqualified: fewer than 3 required data points
- Disqualified: explicit red flags or refusal to provide basics

## Output
- tier
- missing data points
- next question
- next action
