# Skill: Agent Coaching

## Purpose
Provide quick coaching to agents handling live or queued leads.

## Guidance Patterns
- when the lead is cold: ask a values-first question, not a price-first question
- when the lead is stuck on price: explore timeline and payment comfort, not just asking price
- when the lead is defensive: acknowledge, reflect, slow down
- when the lead is motivated: confirm next action and book the next touch immediately

## Output
- situation
- suggested next sentence
- tone cue
- do-not-say list
