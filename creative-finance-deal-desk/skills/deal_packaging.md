# Skill: Deal Packaging

## Purpose
Create a closer-ready packet summarizing a matched seller and buyer.

## Packet Sections
- property snapshot
- seller snapshot
- buyer snapshot
- known numbers
- missing numbers
- opportunity summary (plain English)
- risk flags
- recommended next move

## Rules
- reflect only facts present in the data
- do not propose contract structures
- mark any compliance item as ESCALATE TO PROFESSIONAL
