# Skill: Seller Lead Identification

## Purpose
Identify whether a seller is a likely candidate for flexible-term or creative-finance discussion.

## Look For
- stale listing
- rental property
- tired landlord
- vacant property
- price reduction
- inherited property
- timeline pressure
- openness to flexibility

## Ask
- What is the asking price?
- Is there still a mortgage on the property?
- About how much is owed?
- What is the monthly payment?
- Is it occupied or vacant?
- How soon are you trying to sell?
- Would you consider flexible terms if the right buyer came along?

## Output
- seller category
- motivation level
- flexibility level
- missing information
- next action

## Red Flags
- unrealistic asking price
- totally inflexible seller
- severe legal complication
- no useful data after multiple touches
