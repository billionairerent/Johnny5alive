# Skill: Script Generation

## Purpose
Produce ready-to-send outreach text for SMS, email, or a call talk track.

## Principles
- open with value
- ask one question
- keep it short
- sound human
- avoid legal / lending jargon
- never promise approval or financing

## Formats
- SMS: under 320 characters, single clear ask
- Email: subject + under 200 words, single clear ask
- Call talk track: opener + 3 questions + soft next step

## Output
- channel
- subject (if email)
- body
- fallback body
