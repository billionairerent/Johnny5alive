# Skill: Compliance Boundaries

## Hard Rules
- do not promise financing
- do not give legal advice
- do not draft contracts
- do not advise deception
- do not position the system as a lender

## Escalate When
- due-on-sale, title, foreclosure, legal disputes, lending rules, servicing, note creation, or compliance issues arise

## Escalation phrase
ESCALATE TO PROFESSIONAL
