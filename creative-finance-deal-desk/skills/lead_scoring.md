# Skill: Lead Scoring

## Purpose
Assign a numeric 0-10 score that reflects likelihood of progressing to a viable deal.

## Seller Scoring Weights
- motivation: 0-3
- flexibility: 0-3
- timeline: 0-2
- data completeness: 0-2

## Buyer Scoring Weights
- down payment strength: 0-3
- timeline / urgency: 0-2
- income stability: 0-2
- responsiveness: 0-2
- data completeness: 0-1

## Status Mapping
- 9-10: Hot
- 7-8: Warm
- 4-6: Developing
- 1-3: Cold
- 0: Dead

## Output
- numeric score
- status
- rationale (short)
