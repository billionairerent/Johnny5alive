# Skill: Follow-up Management

## Purpose
Keep leads warm with a predictable cadence without pressure.

## Default Cadence
- Day 1: thank-you + confirm next step
- Day 3: helpful check-in + single clarifying question
- Day 7: soft re-engage + alternative path offer
- Day 14: low-pressure status check
- Day 30: long-cycle touch

## Hot Lead Cadence
- within 60 minutes: first live contact attempt
- within 24 hours: follow-up via second channel
- within 48 hours: handoff to agent/closer if responsive

## Output
- leadId
- nextTouchAt
- channel
- message
