# Skill: Buyer Lead Identification

## Purpose
Identify whether a buyer is likely to be a workable non-bank buyer candidate.

## Look For
- real down payment
- stable income
- urgent move timeline
- self-employed or nontraditional income
- trouble qualifying with a bank
- strong desire to buy soon

## Ask
- What area are you looking in?
- What price range fits you?
- How much do you have for down payment?
- About what is your monthly income?
- How soon do you want to move?
- Are you open to buying directly from an owner instead of through a bank?

## Output
- buyer readiness
- strength of down payment
- urgency
- missing information
- next action

## Red Flags
- no down payment
- unrealistic budget
- vague or inconsistent answers
- expects guaranteed approval
